Vampires

A supernatural race possessing enhanced physical capabilities, abnormal regenerative properties, and abilities centered around predation.

Written by Han Jue
Race
Vampires are considerably more dangerous than ordinary humans in direct combat. Their known traits include enhanced strength, increased vitality, rapid regeneration, blood feeding, heightened senses, and specialized movement abilities.

Racial Traits

Enhanced Strength
  • Deals significantly increased physical damage.
  • High damage output is present even without heavy investment into the Strength stat.
Enhanced Vitality
  • Vampires naturally possess increased maximum HP.
  • Maximum health may vary between vampires despite identical Vitality levels.
  • Observed: 7 Vitality → 167 HP (HUMAN) / 187 HP (VAMPIRE).
Regeneration
  • Possesses extremely powerful natural regeneration.
  • Severe damage can be recovered from rapidly.
  • Makes prolonged engagements extremely difficult.
  • Current observations suggest conventional attrition is ineffective.
Blood Feeding
  • Vampires can bite living targets and consume blood.
  • The bite inflicts considerable damage.
  • Feeding restores or satisfies Hunger.
  • Observed: One bite reduced 167 HP → 121 HP.
Predatory Sense
  • Functions similarly to close-range ESP.
  • Allows nearby entities to be detected without relying entirely on sight.
  • Effective range appears relatively limited.

Mobility

Gliding
  • Certain vampires are capable of gliding.
  • Allows increased aerial control and improved traversal.
  • Currently unknown whether this ability is universal.
Climbing
  • Certain vampires possess enhanced climbing ability.
  • Capable of scaling buildings and vertical surfaces with relative ease.
  • Currently unknown whether this ability is universal.

Notes

  • Vampire abilities may differ between individuals.
  • Gliding and Climbing have not yet been confirmed as universal racial traits.
  • Regeneration combined with feeding makes vampires extremely difficult to defeat through prolonged combat.
  • Additional racial abilities may remain undocumented.
Threat Advisory

Do not assume severe injury means a vampire has been neutralized. Their regenerative capabilities can allow them to rapidly return to combat. Confirm neutralization or disengage.
